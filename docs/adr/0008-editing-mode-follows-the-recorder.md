# Editing Mode decides edits by the Recorder, checked in one pure rule

A Group's Editing Mode is Open (any Member edits or deletes any Expense or Settlement, as before) or Own Records Only. In Own Records Only, a Member may edit or delete an Expense, or delete a Settlement, only if they are its Recorder; the Group Creator may change everything; a record with no Recorder (recorded before Recorders existed, with no Activity to tell) is the Group Creator's alone. Adding Expenses and Settlements is never restricted. Only the Group Creator changes the Editing Mode, and every Member sees it. Existing Groups, and Guest sample Groups that predate it, are Open. The Guest counts as Group Creator of every session Group, so Own Records Only never restricts a Guest.

Who may change a record follows its Recorder, who never changes, rather than the Payer, who an edit can change: otherwise a Member could make a record theirs by editing it, or lose it to someone else's edit. Records with no Recorder go to the Group Creator rather than to everyone, so Own Records Only has no loophole, and rather than to no one, so nothing is left that can never be corrected.

One pure function, `recordChangeBlock(group, visitor, action, target)`, answers whether a change is allowed or why not. The save-Expense and settle-up use cases call it against the Group as stored now, so a stale page or hand-crafted request is refused with the reason and leaves the Group unchanged; the Group page calls the same function to show a record read-only, or without Delete, with that reason. A Group whose Group Creator deleted their account has no Group Creator, so in Own Records Only its unrecorded records, and its Editing Mode, can no longer be changed; we accept that rather than promote another Member.

Own Records Only also makes managing the Group the Group Creator's alone: its details (name, description, Group Currency), adding, removing and inviting Members, giving them an email, and its Custom Categories. Otherwise a Member barred from someone's records could remove them, or change what the Group is. A second pure function, `groupManagementBlock(group, visitor, action)`, answers for each management action; the Group management and Custom Category use cases re-check it against the Group as stored, and Group settings uses it to hide or disable those controls with the reason. So a Group with no Group Creator left, in Own Records Only, can no longer be managed at all, for the same reason as above.
