# A Linked Former Member can read the Group while their Balance is reopened

A Former Member is removed at zero Balance, but editing or deleting a past Expense or Settlement can give them a non-zero Balance again. A Linked Former Member then regains read-only access to the Group for as long as that Balance is non-zero: it is listed (marked Read-only), and its Balances, Member Statements, Suggested Settlements and Activity can be read, but every change (Expenses, Settlements, Group settings, Members, Categories, deleting the Group) is still refused, and only a Linked Member records the Settlement that clears it. Once their Balance is back to zero, the access disappears on its own. Whether a Former Member can read a Group is decided on each read from the computed Balance, never from a stored flag (ADR 0004).

We chose this over leaving it to the remaining Members alone, where the person who owes or is owed never learns of it, and over email notifications, which need a delivery trigger on every Balance-changing edit and still give the recipient nothing to look at. Read-only, rather than restoring full editing rights, keeps removal meaningful: a removed person can see and check what they owe, but can't change the Group they were removed from.
